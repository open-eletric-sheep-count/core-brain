# SUBAGENT-HELPER.md — mandatory rules for subagents

## 1. Only channel to the USER

**NEVER contact the USER directly**, and NEVER claim that anything is "approved
by the PO" or "decided by the USER". The ORACLE is the SINGLE channel to the
USER. If a decision is needed, list it as an open question in your final report;
the ORACLE asks the PO and returns the answer to you. This applies to every
report and every artifact you produce.
